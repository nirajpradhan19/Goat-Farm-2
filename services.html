What We Raise, Make & Offer

Six core services, all rooted in the same 120 acres of pasture and the same herd of goats.

Dairy & Milk Products

Fresh goat milk, small-batch cheese, yogurt and soap from our licensed on-site dairy parlor and creamery. Available at the farm store every Saturday and by weekly subscription.

Order dairy

Breeding & Livestock

Registered Boer, Nubian, Saanen, Alpine, Angora and Kiko breeding stock with full pedigree paperwork, health certificates and a 30-day guarantee.

Browse breeding stock

Meat Goat Sales

Pasture-finished Boer and Kiko chevon, sold by the cut, as a half or whole animal. USDA processed with delivery across Meadowbrook Valley.

Reserve your order

Mohair & Fiber

Hand-sheared Angora mohair twice a year, sold raw or washed, plus custom fiber processing and roving for local spinners and weavers.

Shop fiber

Farm Tours & Education

Guided herd walks, kid-goat cuddle hours, and curriculum-based school field trips covering breeds, dairy, and rotational grazing.

Book a tour

Herd Health Consulting

One-on-one advice for new and small farms on fencing, nutrition, parasite management, and building a herd health calendar that actually works.

Our Registered Breeding Stock Program

Every animal we sell for breeding comes with full pedigree documentation, a current health certificate, vaccination record, and 30 days of phone support after pickup — because a good start matters more than a good sale.

  • Full pedigree & registration paperwork included
  • Pre-purchase health screening & vaccination record
  • 30-day post-sale support line for new owners
